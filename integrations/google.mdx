---
title: Google Drive
description: Connect Google Drive, read Docs and Sheets, and optionally let Flow AI update spreadsheet cells.
---

Connect your Google account to bring Drive files, Google Docs, and Google Sheets into Flow AI. Each person connects their own account. A workspace admin controls the default browsing folder and Sheets write-back setting.

## What you can do

| Capability | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Browse Drive | Start from the mounted folder, or ask for another folder your Google account can access. |
| Read documents | Bring Google Docs content into an agent conversation. |
| Read spreadsheets | Read Google Sheets and reference them through Google Sheet fields. |
| Link documents | Reference a Google Doc through a Google Doc field. |
| Update spreadsheet cells | Enable **Google Sheets write-back** and authorize Sheets write access. Drive and Docs remain read-only. |

## Before you start

- Google Drive must be configured for your Flow workspace. If connection setup is unavailable, contact your Flow administrator.
- Connect a Google account that can access the intended files.
- A Flow admin with access to all projects manages the shared folder scope and write-back setting.
- Flow AI must be enabled to use the connection from an agent.

The Google authorization requests your identity and read access to Drive, Docs, and Sheets. With Sheets write-back enabled, connecting or reconnecting requests Sheets write access instead of Sheets read-only access.

<Note>
  Your connected Google account's permissions determine which content Flow can read. Mounting a folder sets the default browsing location and scopes picker searches; it does not prevent direct reads of other Docs, Sheets, or folders your account can access. Sheets writes are checked against the configured restrictions separately.
</Note>

## Connect Google Drive

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Google account">
    **Requires you to act:** Open **Settings → Integrations → Google Drive** and select **Connect Google**.

    {/* ![Connect a Google account in Flow](images/google-connect.png "human-intervention: connect Google") */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Flow">
    **Requires you to act:** Sign in with the intended Google account and review the requested permissions. Approve access to return to Flow.

    The connection card shows **Connected to Google** and the account email. Each teammate completes this step with their own account, including read-only members who need to connect.

    {/* ![Authorize Flow with Google](images/google-authorize.png "human-intervention: authorize Flow in Google") */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the shared folder scope">
    **Requires you to act:** As a Flow admin, review **Drive scope**. Select **Choose folder to mount**, browse to the intended folder, and select **Mount this folder** to set the default browsing location.

    The folder choice applies across the workspace. Each connected person still needs Google access to that folder. Use Google permissions to restrict what the account can read.

    {/* ![Choose a Google Drive folder in Flow](images/google-scope.png "human-intervention: choose the Drive scope") */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Enable Sheets write-back

**Requires you to act:** As a Flow admin, enable **Google Sheets write-back** to allow the agent to insert and update cell values within the configured Drive scope.

{/* ![Enable Google Sheets write-back](images/google-write-back.png "human-intervention: enable Google Sheets write-back") */}

**Requires you to act:** If your account card shows **Reconnect for Sheets**, select it and approve the additional Sheets permission in Google.

{/* ![Reconnect Google for Sheets write access](images/google-reconnect.png "human-intervention: authorize Google Sheets write access") */}

The connected account must also have edit access to the target sheet. Write-back changes the sheet in Google directly; reverting a Flow branch does not undo those edits.

## Verify the connection

Ask Flow AI to list files in the configured folder and summarize a known Google Doc. Confirm that the result comes from the intended account and folder.

For Sheets, ask the agent to read a known range. To verify write-back, explicitly request a change to a disposable cell in a test sheet and check the resulting value in Google Sheets.

## Change the scope

**Requires you to act:** Use **Change folder** to select a different default folder. Review any separately configured file or folder restrictions as well; changing the mount does not clear them.

{/* ![Change the mounted Google Drive folder](images/google-change-scope.png "human-intervention: change the Drive scope") */}

Removing a mounted folder restores browsing from the Drive root. Any separately configured restrictions still apply to picker searches and Sheets writes. It does not disconnect Google.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The folder picker is disabled">
    Connect your own Google account first. Changing the shared folder requires a Flow admin.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A file is missing or permission is denied">
    Check that the connected Google account can open the file. If it is missing from a picker, check the mounted folder and any separately configured restrictions. A shared workspace connection does not share another person's Google permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Reading works, but writing to Sheets fails">
    Check the workspace write-back setting, the target sheet's edit permissions, and whether the account card asks you to reconnect for Sheets. The sheet must be within the configured Drive scope.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Google reports that the connection is not configured">
    Ask your Flow administrator to confirm that Google authorization is configured. Retry connecting after the configuration is available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Disconnect Google Drive

**Requires you to act:** Select **Disconnect** on your account card to remove your personal connection. Other connected users and the shared folder scope remain in place.

{/* ![Disconnect a personal Google account](images/google-disconnect.png "human-intervention: disconnect your Google account") */}

**Requires you to act:** To remove the shared setup, a Flow admin selects **Remove workspace integration**. Personal Google connections remain stored separately; each person can disconnect their own account.

{/* ![Remove the Google workspace integration](images/google-remove-workspace.png "human-intervention: remove the Google workspace integration") */}
