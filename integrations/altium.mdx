---
title: Altium
description: Connect your Altium 365 account so Flow AI can read PCB projects, schematics, components, and BOMs.
---

Connect your own Altium 365 account to give Flow AI access to the design data you can read through Nexar. Each person connects separately.

The integration is read-only. Flow cannot change your Altium designs.

## What you can do

| Capability | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Find PCB projects | Ask the agent to find projects available to your Altium 365 account. |
| Read design context | Inspect available schematic and component data. |
| Read BOMs | Ask about a project's bill of materials and narrow the request to the parts you need. |
| Respect account access | Every query uses your personal Altium connection. |

The connection does not give colleagues access to your account. Another person's connection does not enable Altium tools for you.

## Before you start

- You need an Altium 365 account with access to the design data you want to read.
- Your Flow installation must have the Nexar connection configured. If Flow shows that Altium is not set up, ask your Flow administrator to configure it.
- Flow AI must be enabled for agent access.
- Read-only Flow members can connect their own Altium account; a workspace admin is not required for personal authorization.

<Note>
  Availability depends on the Nexar configuration for your Flow installation. The connection page reports whether setup is available.
</Note>

## Connect Altium

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start your personal connection">
    **Requires you to act:** Open **Settings → Integrations → Altium** and select **Connect Altium**.

    Flow redirects you to the authorization flow for your Altium account.

    {/* ![Start a personal Altium connection](images/altium-connect.png "human-intervention: connect Altium") */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize your account">
    **Requires you to act:** Sign in with the Altium 365 account you want Flow to use and complete the consent shown by the provider.

    Choose the account that can read your intended projects. Flow cannot grant your account additional Altium permissions.

    {/* ![Authorize the Altium connection](images/altium-authorize.png "human-intervention: authorize Altium access") */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connected account">
    **Requires you to act:** Return to Flow and check that the card shows **Connected to Altium**. Confirm the account email when shown.

    If the connection displays a **reconnect after** time, plan to reconnect after that time. This appears when the authorization cannot refresh automatically.

    {/* ![Confirm the connected Altium account](images/altium-connected.png "human-intervention: verify the Altium account") */}
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify the connection

Ask Flow AI to list PCB projects available through your Altium account. Then ask it to inspect a known project and read a small part of its BOM.

Compare the project and component details with Altium 365. Start with a project you can already open in Altium.

For a large design, name the project and the specific components or BOM section you need. A narrower question avoids oversized results.

## Access and changes

Altium permissions continue to apply after connection. If your account loses access to a project, connecting Flow does not restore it.

There is no Altium write-back setting. Requests to modify a design must be completed in Altium.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Flow says Altium is not set up">
    Ask your Flow administrator to configure the Nexar connection for your installation. Personal sign-in cannot complete until that setup is available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Authorization fails or the connection expires">
    Start the connection again and complete the sign-in flow. If a connected card shows a reconnect time, reconnect after expiry. If the same authorization error persists, give your administrator the error shown in Flow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The agent cannot find a project">
    Check the connected account and confirm it can read that project in Altium 365. Use the project's name or identifier to narrow the request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A large query fails">
    Ask for fewer fields, one project, or a smaller BOM section. Flow limits the size of an individual Altium result.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A colleague cannot use my connection">
    Each person must connect their own Altium account. Agent reads use the requesting person's access.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Disconnect Altium

**Requires you to act:** Select **Disconnect** on your **Connected to Altium** card.

{/* ![Disconnect your Altium account](images/altium-disconnect.png "human-intervention: disconnect Altium") */}

This removes your personal connection from Flow. It does not disconnect colleagues or change any design data in Altium.
