A comparison shows what differs between two states of the project: two branches, a branch and one of its baselines, or two points in one branch's history. Nothing is applied. A comparison between two branches includes differences from both histories; to see only what a branch would merge, open its review instead.

## Choose the two sides

1. Open the branch selector's actions menu, or a branch row's menu, and choose **Compare with...**.
2. On each side, pick a **Branch** and a **Point**. The **Point** list opens on **Current** and the branch's baselines. To start from a single change instead, choose **Changes up to …** on one of them and pick a change; the changes are listed newest first and you can search them. Put the starting state on the left and the resulting state on the right.
3. Open **Compare changes** and select an item to see its changed fields and relationships.

<Illustration name="comparing-what-a-comparison-includes" width={600} alt="A proposal made changes A and B; Base has since made change C. The proposal's review shows only A and B, what would merge. Comparing the two branches now also shows C." />

Comparing **Base** at the change just before the proposal branched (choose **Changes up to …**) with **Proposal** at **Current** shows changes A and B. Comparing **Base** at **Current** with **Proposal** at **Current** also shows change C, from the other side. Open the proposal's review to see only what it would merge: A and B.

Use the swap control to reverse the two sides. Filters and display options narrow the list; clear them to see the full difference.

To compare a single item, open its menu and choose **Compare with branch...**. Its **Point** list offers the same positions, limited to that item's changes.

**Overview** describes the point on the right side: who created a baseline and when, or who merged a branch, along with its discussion.

Baselines stay fixed. A side set to **Current** follows the branch as it changes. If Flow reports that a comparison has expired, choose **Reload comparison**. To capture a state you can come back to, see [Baselining](/branching/baselining).

## See differences in a view

You can also see a branch's differences without leaving a table.

- In **Display options**, turn on **Show changed in branch**. Changed values show above their previous values, and created, updated, and reordered items are tagged. Tree and Reader mark changed items. It is not available on Base.
- In **Choose columns...**, choose **Add field**, then **Field in another branch…**, and pick a branch and a field. The read-only column shows that field as the other branch has it for each row. An item the other branch does not hold reads "Not in" and the branch's name. The column stays on its branch when you switch branches, and is saved with the view.

## Access

Viewers can open comparisons. External recipients can compare only the current state of branches shared with them, limited to the items and fields their share makes visible; points in history and **Overview** are not available to them.

Choosing a side does not switch your working branch. Check the branch selector before you go back to editing.
