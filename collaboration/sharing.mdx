A share gives people or groups access from a starting item through a saved view. The view's structure and relationships decide which connected items they can reach. Use it to bring a supplier, a partner, or another team into exactly the part of the system they need, with the same context your own team works from.

Admins can share. Editors can too when **Settings → Organization → Share view permissions** is set to **Admins and editors**.

## Share access

Link options is expanded with This branch only and No expiry. The submit button reads Share 2 recipients.
</VisualPlaceholder>

1. Open an item's menu and choose **Share**, or select **Share** in the content toolbar. The **Share view** dialog opens.
2. Under **Invite people**, add workspace members, groups, or email addresses. Choose **Can view** for reading and discussion, or **Can edit** to edit the items they reach.
3. Under **Shared destination**, pick the items, **Branches**, and **Views** they will open. Each list takes several picks, and every recipient gets every combination. An item that does not exist on a picked branch is skipped there, and the dialog names it.
4. Expand **Link options**. Choose **This branch only**, **This branch & children**, or **All branches**, and set an expiry if needed. The defaults are **This branch only** and no expiry.
5. Optionally turn on **Show path above** when sharing one item, branch, and view. Recipients also see the items above the shared one, each on its own, without their other children.
6. Check the scope, then select **Invite** or **Share**.

Recipients already in your workspace get an email that links to what you shared; new email addresses get an invitation. **Copy link**, **Who has access**, and **Show path above** work for a single destination only.

External recipients never see private fields. Discussions follow the shared scope.

## What recipients can do

Recipients open the shared views and filter them. They can comment on the items they reach, and open a review for a branch the share covers to discuss it and approve or request changes. Changed items and diffs stay limited to what the share reaches. A **Can edit** share does not let them merge or change review settings.

External recipients can also connect an MCP client, such as an AI assistant, from **Settings → API & MCP** with their own API key. Tool calls reach only what the share reaches, and some operations are not available to external users. Revoking the share removes that access too. See [Connect a client](/api/mcp/connect).

## Maintain the boundary

<VisualPlaceholder kind="Screenshot" title="Shares settings">
  Settings → Shares with a Branch state filter chip set to Merged, two rows whose branch shows its name with a Merged label, both rows selected, and the selection bar at the bottom of the table showing 2 selected and Revoke.
</VisualPlaceholder>

**Who has access** in the dialog lists the grants for a single destination, where you can revoke them. **Settings → Shares** lists every grant in the project, including expired ones. Editors who can share see the grants they created.

1. Open **Settings → Shares**.
2. Search, or select **Filter** to narrow by who shared, branch, **Branch state**, view, access, status, expiry, or when the share was opened or created. A share on a merged or closed branch shows the branch name with a **Merged** or **Closed** label.
3. Select rows, then choose **Revoke** in the bar at the bottom of the table.

What a recipient can reach is worked out on each branch. Editing items, relationships, or the saved view can change it, so recheck grants after restructuring. To change a share's path above, use **Add path above**, **Update path above**, or **Remove path above** on its row.

Other grants, group membership, or project membership can still give someone access; a narrow share never reduces access they already have.

To send teammates a link to the right context, see [Sharing views](/collaboration/sharing-views).
