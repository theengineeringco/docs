A share gives people or groups access from a starting item through a saved view. The view's structure and relationships decide which connected items they can reach. Use it to bring a supplier, a partner, or another team into exactly the part of the system they need.

Admins can share. Editors can too when **Settings → Organization → Share view permissions** is set to **Admins and editors**.

## Share access

<VisualPlaceholder kind="Screenshot" title="Share view dialog" />

1. Open an item's menu and choose **Share**, or select **Share** in the content toolbar. The **Share view** dialog opens.
2. Under **Invite people**, add workspace members, groups, or email addresses. Choose **Can view** for reading and discussion, or **Can edit** to edit the items they reach.
3. Under **Shared destination**, check the items, **Branches**, and **Views** they will open. Each recipient gets every combination you pick.
4. Expand **Link options**. Choose **This branch only**, **This branch & children**, or **All branches**, and set an expiry if needed. The defaults are **This branch only** and no expiry.
5. Optionally turn on **Show path above** when sharing one item, branch, and view. Recipients also see the items above the shared one, each on its own, without their other children.
6. Check the scope, then select **Invite** or **Share**.

External recipients never see private fields. Discussions follow the shared scope.

## Maintain the boundary

**Who has access** in the dialog lists the grants for a single destination, where you can revoke them. Admins can review every grant in **Settings → Shares**.

What a recipient can reach is worked out on each branch. Editing items, relationships, or the saved view can change it, so recheck grants after restructuring. To change a share's path above, use **Update path above**.

Revoke grants you no longer need. Other grants, group membership, or project membership can still give someone access; a narrow share never reduces access they already have.

To send teammates a link to the right context, see [Sharing views](/collaboration/sharing-views).
