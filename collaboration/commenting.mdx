---
title: "Commenting"
description: "Keep engineering discussion attached to the item or passage it concerns."
---

Comments keep a discussion next to the information it is about, so the question, the answer, and the item stay together. Comments belong to a branch; check the branch selector before you post.

## Start or reply to a discussion

1. Open an item's details and select **Discussions**.
2. Post your question. Name the field or discrepancy and the decision you need.
3. Reply in the same thread. Type `@` to mention someone; they are notified according to their [notification settings](/collaboration/subscriptions-notifications).

In **Reader**, hover over an item and choose **Add a comment**. Threads sit in the margin when the window is wide enough and open from the item's header when it is narrow. To discuss exact wording, select text in a rich-text field and comment on the selection.

## Resolve a discussion

1. Record the outcome in the thread.
2. Choose **Resolve comment** on the first comment of the thread. Choose **Unresolve comment** if more work is needed.

Resolving a thread does not change any data. Apply the agreed change to the item as well.

You can edit or delete your own comments.

## Who can take part

Workspace viewers can comment, reply, and resolve threads without gaining edit access to the data. External recipients see and join discussions on the items their [share](/collaboration/sharing) gives them.
