Comments keep a question, its answer, and the item it is about in one place, so people and agents reading the item later see the reasoning behind it. Comments belong to a branch; check the branch selector before you post.

## Start or reply to a discussion

<VisualPlaceholder kind="Screenshot" title="An item's discussion">
  The Discussions tab on the Coastdown Test item: a question asking whether it was rerun on Prototype Build 3, a reply that @mentions the owner, a thumbs-up reaction chip with a count, and the reply box open under the thread. The Resolve comment control is visible on the first comment.
</VisualPlaceholder>

1. Open an item's details and select **Discussions**.
2. Post your question. Name the field or discrepancy and the decision you need.
3. Reply in the same thread. Type `@` to mention someone; they are notified according to their [notification settings](/collaboration/subscriptions-notifications). A comment can hold only a mention.

Unresolved threads keep their reply box open. Long threads show the latest two replies and fold earlier ones into a **Show N replies** row.

Comments take the same rich text as descriptions. A URL you paste becomes a link, and pasting a URL over selected text links that text. Choose **Add reaction** to react with an emoji; reactions do not send notifications.

## Comment on a passage in Reader

<VisualPlaceholder kind="Screenshot" title="Commenting in Reader">
  Reader in the wide layout with a highlighted sentence, the Add a comment button that appears on hover, and an open thread in the margin beside it.
</VisualPlaceholder>

In **Reader**, hover over an item and choose **Add a comment**. Threads sit in the margin when the window is wide enough and open from the item's header when it is narrow. To discuss exact wording, select text in a rich-text field and comment on the selection.

If the quoted text later changes, the thread stays and shows **Referenced text changed** above its quote.

## Link to a comment

Open a comment's **More options** menu and choose **Copy link**. The link opens the item, or the review the comment sits on, with the comment expanded and highlighted. It grants no access; recipients need access to the item already. Read-only members can copy links too.

## Hand a comment to Flow

On a branch with AI turned on, choose **Ask Flow** from an unresolved comment's **More options** menu, or from the thread card on a review. Flow reads the thread, proposes a plan for your approval, makes the change on that branch, and replies in the thread with what it did. Check the result, then resolve the thread.

**Ask Flow** is not offered on Base, on resolved threads, or to read-only and external users.

## Resolve a discussion

1. Record the outcome in the thread.
2. Choose **Resolve comment** on the first comment of the thread. Choose **Unresolve comment** if more work is needed.

Resolving a thread does not change any data. Apply the agreed change to the item as well; the change itself is recorded on the branch, as [Tracing history](/branching/tracing-history) explains.

You can edit or delete your own comments.

## Who can take part

Workspace viewers can comment, reply, react, and resolve threads without gaining edit access to the data. External recipients see and join discussions on the items their [share](/collaboration/sharing) reaches.
