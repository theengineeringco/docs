Subscriptions decide which item activity reaches you. Notification settings decide how it arrives. Together they keep you aware of changes to the parts of the system you own without watching every edit.

## Follow an item

Select the bell in an item's details, or choose **Subscribe** from the item's menu. Select the bell again, or choose **Unsubscribe**, to stop.

<VisualPlaceholder kind="Screenshot" title="Following an item">
  The Battery Pack item's details with the bell selected and Manage subscribers open, listing the owner marked Owner and one other subscriber.
</VisualPlaceholder>

Subscriptions belong to a branch. Following an item on Base does not follow it on other branches.

Owners are subscribed to their items automatically and cannot unsubscribe while they own them. Open the bell's menu to see and manage who is subscribed. A **Subscribers** field on an item also subscribes the people and groups it names, separately from the bell.

Workspace viewers can subscribe. External recipients cannot subscribe and have no inbox.

## Read and act on notifications

<VisualPlaceholder kind="Screenshot" title="Inbox notifications">
  Inbox → Notifications with the All / Unread tabs, the type filter set to Comments, and Mark all as read. One comment-reply row shows its Reply and Ask agent buttons; a subscribed-item change row shows Open details with Unsubscribe from item in its menu.
</VisualPlaceholder>

1. Open **Inbox** from the sidebar footer. It opens on **Notifications**.
2. Switch to **Unread**, or filter by type or branch.
3. Use the action on a row to take the next step: **Reply** to a comment, **Review** a branch, **Answer agent** for a chat that is waiting on you, or **Open** to see the context.
4. Mark entries as read, or choose **Mark all as read**.

The same actions appear on pop-ups. On a change to an item you follow, **Unsubscribe from item** stops further updates.

With AI turned on, **Ask agent** hands a comment, a review that requests changes, or a failed automation run to the Flow agent. It proposes a plan, and nothing changes until you approve it.

## Choose how you are notified

Open **Settings → Notifications**. Each notification type is listed under AI chats, Automations, Reviews and branches, Comments, or Items. For each one, choose any of **In-app**, **Pop-up**, **Email**, and **Slack** when it is connected. Each channel is set separately; turning off email leaves in-app delivery on.

If you get notifications you do not expect, check ownership, your subscriptions, **Subscribers** fields, mentions, and threads you are part of. Unsubscribing from the bell does not remove you from those other audiences. If you miss activity, check the branch filter first.
