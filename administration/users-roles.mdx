A role sets what a person can do; project access sets where. Branch protection, editor groups, and item restrictions can narrow both further.

Flow has four roles: **Admin**, **Member**, **Viewer**, and **External**.

## Admin

For the people who configure Flow and decide who can use it.

Admins can do everything a Member can, and also:

- Invite and remove users, change roles, and grant or revoke project access.
- Configure the data model, shared views, project settings, and review rules.
- Configure integrations and AI settings.
- Read the [audit log](/administration/audit-log) and [AI usage](/administration/usage-billing).

An Admin's rights apply only within the projects they can access. Workspace-wide settings, such as [user groups](/administration/user-groups), [SSO and SCIM](/administration/sso-scim), and granting **All projects**, need an Admin with access to all projects.

## Member

For engineers who build and maintain the model day to day.

Members can work on any project they can access:

- Create, edit, and delete items, field values, and links.
- Create branches, take part in reviews, and merge when branch rules and review conditions allow.
- Comment, subscribe to items, and save private views.
- Use the AI features your organization has turned on.

Members cannot change roles, project access, the data model, shared views, or administrative settings. They can share items with people outside the workspace only when **Share view permissions** in **Settings → Organization** is set to **Admins and editors**.

## Viewer

For stakeholders who follow progress and give feedback without changing the model.

Viewers can read everything in the projects they can access. They can also:

- Comment, reply, and resolve threads.
- Submit or clear their own review decision on a branch.
- Subscribe to items, manage their notifications, and save private views.

Viewers cannot edit items, create branches, merge, or run automations. A review decision does not grant editing access.

## External

For suppliers, customers, and partners who need a defined part of a project.

External users see only what is [shared](/collaboration/sharing) with them; the rest of the workspace appears empty. A share is either **Can view** or **Can edit**. On a shared branch, External users can comment and submit their own review decision, but cannot manage the review or merge. These share levels are separate from the Viewer and Member roles.

## Invite people

1. Open **Settings → Users** and select **Grant Access**.
2. In **Send to**, enter one or more email addresses.
3. Under **Permissions**, choose **Admin**, **Member**, or **Viewer**. Choosing **External** switches to the share dialog, where you pick what to share.
4. Under **Projects**, choose the projects they can open. **All projects** includes projects created later and is offered only to Admins with access to all projects.
5. Select **Send invites**.

People whose email domain requires SSO sign in through your identity provider.

## Change or remove access

- To change one person's projects, open the project list in their **Access** column.
- To act on several people, select their rows and open **Actions**. You can also right-click a row. The menu offers **Resend invite**, **Project access**, **Set role**, and **Remove user**.
- **Resend invite** applies only to people who have not signed in yet. Removing a user cannot be undone, and Flow asks you to confirm.

Users provisioned over SCIM are deactivated in your identity provider, not here. A deactivated user shows a **Deactivated** tag and keeps their role and project access, so reactivating them restores what they had. See [SSO and SCIM](/administration/sso-scim).
