The **Usage** page shows AI token usage across your workspace, from agent chats to automations running in the background. Use it to see who and what is using AI and to spot changes over time. It requires the Admin role.

## Investigate usage

<VisualPlaceholder kind="Screenshot" title="AI usage">
  Settings → AI → Usage set to Last 30 days: totals, the Daily AI Tokens chart, Tokens by user and Tokens by activity, and the allowance cards. Synthetic values, no prices.
</VisualPlaceholder>

1. Open **Settings → AI → Usage**.
2. Choose **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, or **Last 90 days**. The default is 30 days.
3. Read the totals and the **Daily AI Tokens** trend, then compare **Tokens by user** and **Tokens by activity** to find the source of a change.
4. Open the automation or other AI work behind the change to see what it did.

Figures count tokens, not completed tasks or engineering changes. Days are UTC calendar days, and today's figures are partial. People who have left the workspace stay in historical usage, marked **(removed)**. If the page cannot load data, it says so; a load error is not zero usage, so retry before drawing conclusions.

## Allowances and credits

Where prepaid billing is on, the page also shows **Personal allowance**, **Team allowance**, and **Paid credits**. Allowances show usage against their limit and when they reset. Paid credits never expire.

To buy credits, select **Add credits**, review the amount and payment details, and confirm. Use **Manage billing** for other billing actions. If you are unsure whether a payment went through, check its status before paying again.

Without prepaid billing, the usage dashboard works the same and the credit controls do not appear.
