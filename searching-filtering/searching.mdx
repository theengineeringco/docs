---
title: "Searching"
description: "Jump to any item with search, or narrow the view you are in with Find in view."
---

Use search to jump to an item anywhere in the project. Use **Find in view** to narrow the view already on screen.

## Search the project

1. Select **Search** in the sidebar, or press **Cmd+K** (macOS) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows).
2. Type part of an item's name, or its ID.
3. To look in other branches, switch from **Current branch** to **All branches**.
4. Select a result. A result from another branch opens on that branch.

Flow may also list items under **Related by meaning**. Add filters from the search row to narrow results by type or field. Searching all branches matches names only, so filters are unavailable there. External users search the current branch only.

With the updated variant view, the branch tabs read **Current Variant** or **Current change request**, and **All Variants and change requests**.

With nothing typed, search shows **Recents & favorites** and **Browse**, which follows the project tree. Add an item to your favorites from its menu with **Add to favorites**.

If Flow says "Try a longer search term or an exact ID.", it has not searched yet. Type more, or paste the ID.

## Find in view

1. Select the **Find in view** box in the toolbar, or press **Cmd+F** / **Ctrl+F**.
2. Type a term. The view narrows to matching rows and highlights matches. Your filters and focus still apply.
3. Press **Enter** and **Shift+Enter**, or use the arrows, to move between highlights.
4. Clear the box, or press **Escape**, to restore the rows.

Find in view matches text, numbers, dates, IDs, and links. A row can match on a hidden field without showing a highlight; open the item to see why it matched. Switching saved view or project clears the term. The Architecture tab has no Find in view.

If an item is missing, check your filters and focus, or search **All branches**. Results only include items you can access. To find items by condition, such as a missing owner or a value above a limit, use [Filtering](/searching-filtering/filtering).
