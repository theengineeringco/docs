Filters show only the items that meet the conditions you set. They do not change any records, and filtering a review does not change what a merge includes.

## Filter a view

<VisualPlaceholder kind="Screenshot" title="View filter bar: Owner is empty chip plus an Advanced filter with OR" />

1. Select **Filter** in the toolbar, or open a column's menu and select **Filter on this field**.
2. Choose a field, an operator, and a value.
3. Add more conditions as needed. Separate filter chips must all match.

| To find | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Items with no owner | Owner **is empty** |
| Items at a stage | The stage field, then the stage |
| Values above a limit | A number field, greater than, then the limit |

The fields and operators on offer come from your [data model](/core-concepts/the-data-model). Results stay within the current branch, view, and focus.

To match any of several conditions, add an **Advanced filter**. It starts as **OR**; switch it to **AND** from its operator. For example, a stage chip together with an advanced filter of Owner **is empty** **OR** Description **is empty** finds items at that stage that are missing either one.

Remove a chip to drop its condition. Filter changes apply only for you until you [save the view](/collaboration/sharing-views); **Reset to default** returns to the view's saved filters.

Turn on **Show ancestors of filter results** in the view's display options to keep each match's parents visible for context. Those parents do not have to match.

## Filter a review

On a branch's **Review** page, open **Changed Items** and select **Filter**.

<VisualPlaceholder kind="Screenshot" title="Review → Changed Items filter with Changed by (Any AI) and a field &quot;is changed&quot;" />

| To find | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Added or removed items | **Change type**: Created or Deleted |
| Changes by a person or by AI | **Changed by**: a person, or **Any AI** |
| Edits to one field | The field **is changed** |
| Changed items missing a value | The field **is empty** |

The other change types are Updated, Restored, Linked, and Reordered.

Chips match whole items. Combining **Changed by** with **is changed** finds items that person changed and whose field changed; it does not show that the person made that field edit. Open the change to confirm who made it.

Review filters are remembered per branch in your browser. Clear them before you approve so you see the full set of changes. To look up an item by name, use [Searching](/searching-filtering/searching).
